My name is Sergey Shteingolts, and I am a PhD student at the Technion -- Israel Institute of Technology, working in the [Computational Molecular Engineering Lab](https://mendelsgroup.net.technion.ac.il/) under the supervision of Dr. Dan Mendels.

My research focuses on applying machine learning for molecular dynamics and material science, in particular graph neural network (GNN) surrogates. I study how these models can be trained to predict simulation trajectories and used for inverse design and optimization of systems with desired dynamical properties.

## Research

**GNN-based molecular dynamics simulators.** I develop graph neural network models that learn to reproduce molecular dynamics trajectories directly from structure, with a focus on making these simulators generalize out of distribution, for example to structures, sizes, and conditions not seen during training.

**Inverse design and optimization.** Because the GNN simulators are differentiable, they can be used to optimize a system's structure directly for a target dynamical or mechanical property. This is a priority of my current work, applied to disordered elastic networks: using differentiable graph neural network molecular dynamics to solve out-of-distribution inverse design and optimization problems.

## Earlier work

Before starting my current PhD, I spent five years working on experimental charge density analysis in an X-ray diffraction laboratory. This work used high-resolution X-ray diffraction and multipole refinement to determine the experimental electron density distribution inside small organic and pharmaceutical molecules in the crystalline state, including studies of chemical bonding, noncovalent interactions, and structural disorder. These earlier publications are listed on the [publications page](/publications/).
